About

I’m a content and performance marketer with a background in digital media production. I connect audience understanding, creative direction and paid media with the practical work of getting campaigns and content out into the world.

A strategist with a storyteller’s eye and a hands-on approach to delivery.

My work spans Caribbean and international markets. At Yello, I work across editorial content, social media, email, campaign delivery and reporting. Alongside that, I use AI-assisted workflows to develop creative concepts, reporting tools and practical prototypes.

I’ve also facilitated practical AI and digital-marketing learning in the AI-LIGNED virtual incubator.

I care about humanising the work too, making brands feel more real, relatable, and worth paying attention to.

Outside of work, I stay active through basketball and fitness, and I spend time exploring food, music, and new places, documenting life and culture with the eye of a hobbyist documentary-style storyteller and photographer.

You can see some of this through my Instagram, where I share moments from places, food, and everyday life through a visual storytelling lens.

Focused on outcomes, not activity.

How I Approach the Work

My approach is built around four principles: objective, alignment, optimisation, and data.

Start with a clear purpose

I start with the audience, the problem and the decision or experience the work needs to support.

Audience, message, and channel must align

Performance improves when the offer, creative, and platform match the audience. Strong alignment is what turns activity into results.

Test, learn, and optimise continuously

I test early, review performance closely, and improve the parts that move results, from targeting and creative to landing pages.

Match the evidence to the claim

Campaign results, prototype output and learning questions tell different stories. I keep those distinctions visible as the work develops.
