Marketing operations / Internal platform

Making Invisible Marketing Work Visible

Marketing Contribution & Workload Dashboard

Explore the dashboard ↗

01 Context

A system designed to make marketing contribution, active workstreams, creative systems, prototypes and strategic initiatives easier to see.

02 Problem

Recurring delivery and less visible strategic work need to be understood together without losing their different statuses.

03 Idea / Thinking

Separate the operating rhythm from projects, creative systems, innovation inputs and strategic presentations.

04 My Role

Dashboard development and organisation of marketing contribution, workstreams and strategic initiatives.

05 What I Built / Did

An internal operations dashboard with workload, projects, creative systems, content, innovation, enablement and evidence views.

06 Output

A documented portfolio of 20 recurring workstreams, 19 active/ongoing/live items, 4 creative systems, 10 product/innovation inputs and 7 strategic presentations.

07 Evidence

These are documented scope and inventory counts. They are not hours worked, productivity increases, time savings or adoption measures.

08 Status

Internal operations platform. A structured view of contribution and ongoing work.

09 Learning / Next question

How can scope stay visible without confusing activity counts with value or effort?
