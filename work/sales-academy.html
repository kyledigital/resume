Sales enablement / Prototype

FindYello Sales Academy

Turning product training into practice

Explore the academy ↗

01 Context

An interactive sales training experience designed to help team members understand FindYello products and practise recommending them in realistic customer scenarios.

02 Problem

Knowing product facts and applying them to a customer conversation are different tasks.

03 Idea / Thinking

Organise product knowledge around customer needs, recommendations and short practice sessions.

04 My Role

Concept, learning structure, product interpretation, UX direction and AI-assisted development.

05 What I Built / Did

A learning dashboard, product guide, recommendation lab, refreshers and practice routes.

06 Output

An internal enablement prototype covering 11 products, 10 customer scenarios and 20 practice questions.

07 Evidence

The linked interface shows the product, scenario and question counts. These describe content coverage, not completed training, usage or improved sales performance.

08 Status

Internal enablement prototype. Available to explore; no rollout or adoption claim.

09 Learning / Next question

Which customer scenarios would be most useful to validate with sales colleagues?
