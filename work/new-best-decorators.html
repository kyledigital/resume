Interior décor website / Live website

The New Best Decorators

Making a made-to-measure service easier to explore

Visit the website ↗

01 Context

A service-led website presenting custom drapery, furnishings and interior décor with a guided estimate-request journey.

02 Problem

Customers need to understand the services, picture the possibilities and explain what their space needs.

03 Idea / Thinking

Combine clear service descriptions, visual inspiration and the steps from measurement to installation.

04 My Role

Website copy, service organisation, visual direction and AI-assisted website development.

05 What I Built / Did

Service sections, a filterable inspiration gallery, a process overview and an estimate form that organises project details for WhatsApp review.

06 Output

A live responsive website for the Mandeville business.

07 Evidence

The site presents service information and a structured enquiry. Inspiration imagery is identified on the website; it is not all presented as completed client work.

08 Status

Published website.

09 Learning / Next question

How can the next iteration make the enquiry journey clearer?
