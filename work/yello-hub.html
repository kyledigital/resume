Marketing intelligence / Internal platform

Yello Marketing Intelligence Hub

Turning marketing reporting into a working intelligence platform

Explore the platform ↗

01 Context

A stakeholder-facing interactive platform connecting campaign performance, content strategy, creative direction, UX opportunities, product ideas and marketing priorities.

02 Problem

Campaign data, content priorities and product opportunities need a shared context for stakeholder discussion.

03 Idea / Thinking

Bring the evidence and the strategic questions into one navigable experience, while keeping measurement sources distinct.

04 My Role

Concept development, information architecture, marketing strategy, data organisation, UX direction and AI-assisted web and prototype development.

05 What I Built / Did

A structured stakeholder experience covering performance, strategy, content pillars, sales positioning, training, prototypes, UX opportunities and a roadmap.

06 Output

A working internal platform with a published reporting snapshot, rather than a live Meta or GA4 connection.

07 Evidence

Captured reporting: 233,738 people reached, 788,941 paid impressions, 17,277 Meta link clicks and approximately 6,300 GA4 campaign sessions across six Caribbean markets. Paid Meta scope: 1 June–15 July 2026; organic social and GA4 scope: 1 April–15 July 2026. Sessions are visits, not leads or sales. These figures describe the campaigns in the hub, not the impact of building the platform.

08 Status

Internal platform / evolving. Published reporting snapshot; no adoption or stakeholder outcome claimed.

09 Learning / Next question

How can the next reporting cycle make the connection between evidence and decisions easier to follow?
