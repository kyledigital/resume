Independent publishing / Live product

The Psychology of Jamaicans

Turning an independent book into an interactive cultural platform

Explore the cultural platform ↗

01 Context

An independent project that began as a book and evolved into an interactive cultural platform.

02 Problem

A book offers depth, while a digital experience can offer different ways to explore cultural ideas and contribute observations.

03 Idea / Thinking

Extend the written work through cultural signals, an interactive decoder and the Jamaica Pulse interface.

04 My Role

Author and independent product creator; cultural content development and AI-assisted digital development.

05 What I Built / Did

A 28-chapter book in four sections, a cultural platform, book commerce routes and a community contribution interface.

06 Output

A published book and accessible digital experience, with digital and Amazon print purchase routes.

07 Evidence

The live site shows 28 chapters, four parts, Jamaica Pulse, contribution fields and commerce links. Availability is not evidence of revenue, readership or community participation. Cultural signals are interface content, not portfolio performance metrics.

08 Status

Independent digital product / live website.

09 Learning / Next question

How can a book and an interactive experience offer complementary ways to explore the same cultural ideas?
